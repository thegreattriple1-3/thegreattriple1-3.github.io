Hit your key as each note reaches the line. Hit it and your instrument plays. Miss it and the band goes quiet and the crowd cools off. A note with a long tail is a held note: keep the key (or finger) down until the tail runs out. Full band keys: A S D F for the first four sections, J K L ; for the last four. Solo and Duo turn it into a four-lane rhythm game on one instrument (or two players on two), with the rest of the band as bots. On a touch screen, tap your lane. Each tune has its own style and layout, so listen for who has the lead.
